About

Interior designer & design technologist, based in Toronto.

Carl Moscoso is an interior designer and design technologist based in Toronto, holding a three-year Advanced Diploma in Interior Design Technology from George Brown Polytechnic (2023). His work moves between concept and construction — space planning, presentation boards, and full drawing sets built in AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp, and Rhino, rendered out in Enscape.

Before design, six years in retail and e-commerce sharpened a different set of instincts: accuracy under deadline, composure under pressure, and a habit of seeing things through to the end. He has also built and maintained a brand’s website from the ground up, the same eye for composition and consistency he brings to a floor plan.
